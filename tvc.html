Time-Value Collection (TVC)

TVC adjusts each payout according to its collection position. Under the backend formula, earlier collection positions receive a negative adjustment and later positions receive a corresponding positive adjustment. The midpoint position has no adjustment.

Why TVC is used

A member receiving the pool in the first week obtains access to the pooled contributions earlier than a member receiving it in the fifth week. TVC recognises this difference through a transparent adjustment to the payout amounts. It is not a separate payment collected by Thrifty.

The annual TVC rate is derived by the backend when a savings circle is activated, using the circle's configured contribution and membership settings. The exact annual rate for your circle is displayed in the saver dashboard; there is no fixed universal percentage.

How the calculation works

The sandbox circle has 5 co-savers contributing £200 each week, giving a gross pool of £1,000 before TVC. The platform maintenance fee is 0% during this trial.

1. Gross pool

P = N × M = 5 × £200 = £1,000

2. Pivot position

Pivot = (N + 1) / 2 = (5 + 1) / 2 = 3

3. TVC adjustment

Adjustment = P × (2T − (N + 1)) × r × N

Where T is the co-saver's slot number, N is the number of co-savers, P is the gross pool, and r is the annual TVC rate divided by 104. The annual rate is derived by the backend and shown in the saver dashboard.

4. Adjusted payout, before maintenance

Adjusted Payout = P + Adjustment

5. Platform maintenance fee and final payout

Maintenance Fee = £0.00 (0%)
Final Payout = Adjusted Payout

No platform maintenance fee is deducted during this five-week sandbox trial.

Where the money goes

  • Co-Savers pay contributions directly into the bank account designated by their Community Partner or Community Admin.
  • The Community Admin administers the circle's pool and arranges the payout.
  • No platform maintenance fee is deducted or transferred during the five-week sandbox trial.
  • Thrifty provides the software calculation and records. It does not receive, hold, pool, lend or distribute Co-Savers' contributions or payout funds.

These figures are an illustration of the standard, fully funded circle. A missed or unconfirmed contribution can affect the funds actually available for a payout. See the Terms of Service and the applicable community rules for the responsibilities and procedures that apply.
